Frequently Asked Questions

What does Taskora do?

Taskora helps you stay compliant by protecting your business from risk, explaining regulations clearly, and handling your filings so you don't have to.

Who can use Taskora?

Startups, small businesses and individuals can use Taskora. You can pick the plan that fits the size of your business.

How do I get started?

Create an account from the Sign Up section, choose a plan, and our team will guide you through the next steps.

How long does a filing take?

The time depends on the type of filing and how quickly you share your documents. We will give you an estimate before we begin.

Can I change or cancel my plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade or cancel your plan at any time from your account or by contacting our support team.

Is my data safe with Taskora?

We treat your documents and personal details as confidential and only use them to deliver the services you ask for.

How can I contact support?

Use the Contact Us form on this page or email us, and we will reply as soon as we can.
